# Allow Copy and Paste Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Allow Copy and Paste restores standard browser interactions.

## 1. Implementation
The extension works by re-enabling standard DOM events (like `oncopy`, `oncontextmenu`) that websites may have disabled. It does not intercept or record the content you copy or paste.

## 2. No Data Collection
No user data or browsing history is collected or transmitted.
